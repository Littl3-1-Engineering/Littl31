We tell you what we killed, too.

Most "AI butler" claims in this category are vaporware. Alfr3d's situational-awareness engine has 19 rules live in production — and three more that were investigated and deliberately not shipped, on real evidence, published here before anyone had to ask.

This isn't a compliance page. It's the cheapest, most honest proof of the ownership thesis we have — a system that tells you what it can't do yet is more trustworthy than one that quietly fakes it.

KILLED · SA-9

Room-level sensors from ESPHome

The plan: extend situational awareness into per-room presence and environment data from ESPHome nodes on your network. The integration itself is built through Phase 4 — most of the engineering work is already done.

Why it's not live — there's no real ESPHome node on the household's own network to validate it against. Rather than ship an untested integration wired into zero real rules, this stays parked until real hardware exists to test it on.

Re-entry: the moment a real ESPHome node is reachable on the LAN, this picks back up — most of the work is already sitting there waiting.

KILLED · SA-8

Room-level presence via Bluetooth

The plan: detect which room someone's actually in over BLE, without a camera or microphone in the room — the privacy-respecting way to do presence.

Why it's dead — the household's real Bluetooth adapter is genuinely capable. Two real passive scans found zero stably-identifiable personal devices. Modern phones randomize their BLE MAC address specifically to prevent this kind of tracking — a feature, not a bug, and one Alfr3d isn't going to fight.

Re-entry: not without new hardware that solves identity a different way. It isn't on the roadmap absent that.

STOPPED · SA-12

Predicting what happens next

The plan: move from noticing what's unusual today to actually anticipating what happens next — conditional patterns mined from your own household's history, not a model trained on anyone else's.

Why it's stopped — this needs real history to mine, and the household event log (itself a very recent addition) only held about 10 hours of real data when this was checked. Shipping a "prediction" feature on 10 hours of history would mean faking a confidence it doesn't have.

Re-entry: once the event log holds at least four continuous weeks of real history. This is the single highest-value item on the whole roadmap once it's viable — the actual definition of anticipation, not just noticing.

STANDING RULES

What governs everything that does ship

No fabricated data. If a card can't be computed from something real, it doesn't render — silence over a fake reading, always.

Nothing here actuates anything. Every card in the situational-awareness engine is informational. None of it flips a switch on its own.

Learned state is inspectable and resettable. A system that learns about your household has to be one you can correct or wipe, not a black box.

Observation, never concern. Cards note that something's unusual — they don't ask if you're alright. That's a promise this product can't keep, so it doesn't make it.

Household-local, always. No cross-household aggregation, no upload, no training on your data. What Alfr3d learns about your house stays on your Kit.
