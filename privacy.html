Your data stays where you put it.

Last updated: September 5, 2026.

This policy covers everything Littl3.1 Engineering operates — this website (littl31.com), ALFR3D Core, Alfr3d Deck, and the ALFR3D Kit, Relay, and Alfr3d Uplink services once they launch.

The short version: we don't run ads, we don't sell data, and most of what ALFR3D does happens entirely on hardware you own. Where that's not true — Google Play billing, a future hosted relay — we say so plainly below.

§ 1

No ads, ever

Littl3.1 Engineering does not run advertising, ad networks, or ad-tech tracking on littl31.com, ALFR3D Core, or Alfr3d Deck, and never will. This isn't a pricing decision we might revisit — it's policy. We make money by selling hardware (the ALFR3D Kit) and optional subscriptions (Relay, Alfr3d Uplink), never by selling attention or data.

§ 2

littl31.com — this website

This site doesn't have accounts, forms, or a database behind it. It's a static site with no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no cookies of any kind — we don't know who you are or that you visited.

Every "waitlist" or "notify me" link on the ALFR3D product page opens your own email client via a mailto: link. Nothing is submitted to a server on this site — whatever you send is a normal email to athos@littl31.com, and you're in control of what it contains.

Our hosting providers — GitHub Pages for the site itself, and Cloudflare for the small payments backend described below — generate standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) as a normal part of serving any website. We don't access or use these logs ourselves; see GitHub's and Cloudflare's own privacy policies for how they handle them.

When Stripe checkout goes live for a paid product (it isn't yet — every pricing tier today is a waitlist link, not a working purchase), payment details will be entered directly into Stripe's own checkout page and processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we'll only receive what Stripe tells us is needed to activate your order, such as confirmation a payment succeeded and an email to send a receipt to.

§ 3

ALFR3D Core — the self-hosted backend

ALFR3D Core is software you download and run on your own hardware — a Raspberry Pi, a home server, whatever you choose. There is no Littl3.1-operated server in this picture — your residents, devices, routines, camera streams, and situational-awareness history live in a database that you host, on a network you control. We never see it, store it, or have access to it.

Logging into your own ALFR3D instance uses a password you set, hashed and never stored in plain text; sessions use short-lived access tokens and revocable refresh tokens, all issued by your own instance. Credentials for anything you connect — Home Assistant, SmartThings, an ESPHome device, Spotify, an LLM API key — are encrypted at rest in your own database, not sent to us.

Integrations you configure yourself fall into two categories. Local integrations (Home Assistant, SmartThings, ESPHome devices) talk to your own instance over your own network and never touch the internet. A small number of optional integrations (Spotify, an LLM provider like Anthropic) do go out to the internet — but directly from your own ALFR3D instance to that provider, using credentials only you hold. Littl3.1's servers are never in that path.

Because ALFR3D Core is self-hosted, you're the one in control of retention and deletion — back it up, wipe it, or move it however you like. We can't do any of that for you, because we don't have a copy.

§ 4

Alfr3d Deck — the Android app

Alfr3d Deck is a home-screen replacement for Android, currently in development and not yet published to Google Play. This is the policy that will apply once it ships.

The launcher does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with us or any third party. Contacts, calendar, and file search results are read locally to power the Search Panel and never leave your device. The media-control window reads the OS's active media-session list (title, artist, app), never notification content. The "Lock" action uses Android's on-demand device-lock API only — no other device-admin policy is requested.

The only network requests the launcher makes are to an ALFR3D backend, if and only if you configure one yourself in Settings — the same self-hosted instance described above, under your control — and to your device's default browser when you explicitly choose to search the web.

The one paid feature, Alfr3d Deck Pro, is a one-time unlock purchased through Google Play Billing. Google processes that payment; we receive confirmation that you own the unlock, never your payment details.

App-ranking data (which apps you use most) is stored locally on-device using Android's DataStore and is never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the launcher removes it.

§ 5

ALFR3D Kit, Relay, and Alfr3d Uplink — not yet available

None of these exist yet — the Kit hasn't shipped, and Relay and Alfr3d Uplink haven't been built. Nothing in this section collects any data today; we're publishing our design commitments now so they're on record before launch, and we'll update this policy — with a new "last updated" date, at this same URL — before any of it goes live.

Relay, the planned remote-access layer, is designed as a dumb pipe — an encrypted tunnel between your ALFR3D instance and your own client, relayed through our infrastructure without our servers ever seeing plaintext home data. Alfr3d Uplink adds push notifications, hosted encrypted backups, and personality features on top.

Subscribing to Relay or Alfr3d Uplink will require a billing identity (an email and payment method, handled through Stripe) — deliberately kept separate from the login you use inside your own household's ALFR3D instance. Knowing you're a subscriber won't give us, or anyone at Littl3.1, access to your home data.

§ 6

Children's privacy

Nothing Littl3.1 Engineering operates is directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. Since littl31.com collects nothing and ALFR3D Core runs entirely on hardware the user controls, there's generally nothing here for us to collect from anyone, of any age.

§ 7

Your data, your control

For anything self-hosted — ALFR3D Core, and by extension Alfr3d Deck's optional backend connection — you already hold all the data. Export it, delete it, or move it however you like; we have no copy to act on.

For anything that does touch us directly — an email you send us, a future Stripe subscription — email athos@littl31.com and we'll act on a request to access, correct, or delete it.

Littl3.1 Engineering is a registered business in Ontario, Canada, and this policy applies to visitors and users wherever they're located.

§ 8

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change and the new version will be published at this same URL. Material changes — a new product going live, a new category of data collection — will also get a note in the section they affect.

§ 9
